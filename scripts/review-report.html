Practice your own mistakes

Every position here comes from one of your games. Find the win: you had something much better than what you played. Spot the threat: your move left something open; find how your opponent punishes it. Click a piece, then the square it should go to.

Where the points go

Your mistakes and blunders, sorted by what actually happened on the board. Inaccuracies are left out; they rarely decide games at this level.

Game by game

The line under each game is your winning chances move by move (up is better for you). Open a game to see each flagged move and load it onto the board.
